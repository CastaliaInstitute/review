How a Review Works

Each review proceeds in three movements:

  • Essay — a synthetic account of the work
  • Marginalia — annotated passages with faculty voices (in voce)
  • Reply — final synthesis under a single author of record

Editorial Note

Criticism has become either immediate or specialized. It rarely accepts responsibility for judgment.

Castalia Review proceeds differently. It stages multiple intelligences in dialogue, but does not leave conclusions unresolved. Each review culminates in a synthesis for which a Custodian is accountable.
