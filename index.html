Castalia Review

Most contemporary book discourse is too fast, too partisan, or too shallow for civilizational questions. Castalia Review exists for the books that deserve slower judgment: polyphonic review essays in which several disciplined voices disagree in public—always tethered to the text.

Each piece combines a framing overview, line-level marginalia on quoted passages, and a synthetic afterword (sometimes including a reply staged in the book’s own voice). Technology, politics, and culture are read as questions for the whole person—history, ethics, and imagination together.

How a Castalia Review works

  • 1 · Overview essay The custodian introduces the work and sets the stakes before the faculty sections open.
  • 2 · Faculty marginalia Named reviewers annotate the book passage by passage; superscripts in the essay jump to those notes.
  • 3 · Synthesis and afterword The voices converge or diverge openly; the afterword gathers threads or lets the book answer back.
